Notes from the Desk

The engineer who reads the circuit before the code.

By the Desk Editor · Soshanguve Bureau

Nyiko Evidence Tsonani is studying towards a Diploma in Computer Systems Engineering at Tshwane University of Technology in Soshanguve, where she has been enrolled since January 2022. Colleagues describe her as the kind of student who opens the case to see what is inside, then writes the software to make it behave.

Her work runs across the whole stack of a machine. At the low end she writes Assembly and C++, close to the hardware; further up she builds applications in C# and Java, and she keeps the records straight with SQL. It is an unusual range for one desk, and she is happy to keep both ends of it.

The backend is where trust is built: validate every request, enforce clear business rules, handle errors with meaningful messages and protect the database as the single source of truth.

Nyiko Tsonani, Backend Developer · ARMS

The trail begins at EPP Mhinga Secondary School, where she matriculated after studying there from 2017 to 2021. In 2025 she added the WeThinkCode_ GenAI course to her file, bringing generative AI into a toolkit that already ran from registers to relational tables.

Off the clock, she is a familiar face at student leadership events on campus. She is currently open to internships and graduate opportunities, and this paper understands she replies quickly to any letter that arrives at the Letters desk.

Event report

iFoW — Institute for the Future of Work

Pretoria · Tshwane University of Technology

Nyiko joined fellow students at an event run by the Institute for the Future of Work (iFoW), an initiative of Tshwane University of Technology. Business owners and representatives from companies took the room through how the business world really works.

The speakers shared what it takes to start a business, from turning an idea into an offer to finding the first customers, and what companies look for in the people they hire. For an engineer who builds systems, it was a close look at the businesses those systems are built for, and at the path to one day starting her own.
